# Community

Steading started as a solo project, and is an early-stage group of five.[^governance]

The community is neighbors, not a workforce. No shared product roadmap is imposed on anyone, and no one decides what counts as a worthwhile pursuit. Sometimes people's interests intersect and they build something together; sometimes they don't.[^governance]

[Contributing](contributing.md) says where to join in.
